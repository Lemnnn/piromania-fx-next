# Home (/[lang])

Mode: Persuade. Audience: private, corporate and city clients (see PRODUCT.md). Job: see the shows at true scale, then request a quote.

Pinned by user: "Night sky / immersive" direction; hero modelled on No Art (noartmusic.com) window-opening loader. No image generation in this harness: code-led build. Placeholder media from Unsplash/Mixkit until client footage arrives.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site opens the way a show does: darkness, one spark, then the whole sky. It refuses the category default of a photo slider with stacked service paragraphs.

OWN-WORLD: Piromania's own colours at night: a burgundy-black ground (#140808), warm-white type (#F4ECE8), the logo flame (#EB3D00; buttons #CF3600 for contrast) as the only interface accent, and the brand burgundy (#811410) as the single drenched field (prices) and in the menu sweep. Big Shoulders condensed display (Chicago wood-type lineage, like firework-shell labels) with Geist for text. Square corners throughout; the frame is the motif. (Revised from sodium gold at the client's request to keep the brand reds.)

STORY: Visitors see real fireworks at full bleed within 4 seconds and read "Fantezii explozive" plus who it is for. Every path leads to "Cere ofertă".

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed show video. Bottom-left: the headline at 6rem across 1–2 lines. Bottom-right: a 20-word pitch, the gold "Cere ofertă" button and a "Vezi pachetele" link. A 72px header with the wordmark, five links, RO/EN and the CTA. Intro: a 0–100% counter, a 4rem spark window, a 45vw×25vw frame with the PIROMANIA wordmark behind it, then the full screen. Plays once per session. Revised: the intro opens on the Piromania logo disc, which becomes the spark (the footage opens as a circle in its place), then the 45vw frame with the brand wordmark behind it.

FORM: Pinned direction (user choice), No Art opening grammar; no concept-seed roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
